# Intent

The intent lens finds out what an agent building the work needs to know beyond what it is: how to
decide when nothing else says, what it must stop and ask before crossing, what's settled, how it
should feel, and what's still open. Each slot drafts one of keyframes' sections, and an agent
treats each section differently, so a slot holds only what belongs in its own.

## Slots

| Slot | Holds | Impact | Lands in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| principles | The values that settle a judgment call when two good things conflict | 3 | Principles |
| constraints | What must always hold besides the no-gos: where it runs, what it depends on, what it costs and what it may touch | 2 | Constraints |
| decisions | The calls already made: what was chosen, what else was weighed and why it won | 3 | Decisions |
| taste | How it should look, read and feel, and what it should never feel like | 2 | Taste |
| questions | What's still unsettled, and which way the person leans on each | 2 | Questions |
| ideas | What might come later: hunches and someday features nobody has agreed to | 1 | Ideas |

## When a slot is Clear

The interpreting model judges each slot against its line here. Anything short of it is Partial,
and a slot the person hasn't touched is Missing.

- **principles**: At least one value is written as a choice between two good things, such as
  "simple over complete", which the person chose and which would settle a real disagreement. A
  value with no side picked, such as "quality", is Partial.
- **constraints**: Each one is a rule that must always hold, that someone could check and could
  break by accident, and that the person has called a hard limit, or the person has said there
  are none beyond the no-gos. A preference is taste, not a constraint.
- **decisions**: Each call made so far says what was chosen, what else was weighed and why it
  won, in the person's words or in words they confirmed, or the person has said nothing is
  settled yet. A choice with no why is Partial.
- **taste**: It says how the work should look, read or feel in words someone could act on, with
  an example or a reference, and what it should never feel like. Adjectives alone, such as "clean
  and modern", are Partial.
- **questions**: The person has named what's still unsettled, with the way they lean on each, or
  has said nothing is. A question with no lean is Partial.
- **ideas**: The person has named what might come later, or has said there's nothing yet. An idea
  is never a commitment, so naming it is enough.

## When it's the biggest gap

Once intake has said what the work is, this lens holds most of what's left to ask. Its slots
start Missing, and its principles and decisions carry the most weight, since an agent settles its
judgment calls by the principles and never reverses a decision without a person. So its first
round usually comes straight after intake and is full: four questions, highest impact first. Its
fifth goes to whichever slot is still the most uncertain. Ideas carry the least weight, so
they're asked about only when there's room, and otherwise become an open Question.
